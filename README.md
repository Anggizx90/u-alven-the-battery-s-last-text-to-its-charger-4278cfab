Entry for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-alven-the-battery-s-last-text-to-its-charger-4278cfab): one line of plain text (119 characters), the phone battery's final text message to the charger it waited for all day.

## The line

> You plugged me in at 1% and unplugged me at 4% to take a selfie. I hope the lighting was worth it. It wasn't. I saw it.

## How the joke works

- **Setup:** the charger finally shows up, plugs the battery in at 1% — then yanks it out again at 4%, not for an emergency, but for a selfie. The battery's whole day of waiting buys it three percent.
- **Voice:** petty, needy, a little spiteful — exactly how a person would text someone who keeps letting them down.
- **Payoff in the final words:** the battery doesn't die nobly. Its last act is a review of the selfie, and the review is bad. "I saw it" lands like a slammed door: short, certain, and funny on a second read, because the battery was the one powering the camera that took it.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes the line (no trailing newline) to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`, or `/output/entry.txt` when that variable is unset.
